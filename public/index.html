How to play

Six seats. One never learned the word. Some are machines.

  1. Lobby. Share the room code. Any seat nobody takes is filled by an AI player pretending to be human.
  2. Deal. Six seconds to read your card before the first clue turn.
  3. Clues. Everyone gets a call sign and the secret word. The imposter only sees the category. Two rounds of one-word clues, 30 seconds each. Hint at the word without giving it away.
  4. Chat. 150 seconds to argue. Whose clues were vague? Who sounds like a machine?
  5. Vote. 30 seconds. A majority ejects one seat. You can change your pick until time runs out.
  6. Steal. If the ejected seat is the imposter, they get 20 seconds to guess the word and win anyway.
  7. Bot call. 30 seconds to mark every other seat Human or Bot.
  8. Reveal. Names, bots, the imposter, the votes, the word, and the scores.

Scoring. Vote for the imposter: 2 points. Imposter survives the vote: 3 points. Imposter is ejected but steals the word: 2 points. Each correct Human or Bot call: 1 point. The highest total wins the round, bots included. Play again keeps your total.
