The curated self vs. the actual self
Explanation
Alex is making these recordings for aliens who will never meet him, and he's copying the Voyager Golden Record, a portrait of Earth with the war and cruelty removed. That makes every chapter a performance, and the book keeps reminding you that someone is choosing what goes in. The question it raises is whether that choice is a message or a lie, and whether Alex knows he's making it. 
Example
The gap after the hospital. Once his mom is taken in and Alex is in one of his toughest moments, the recordings stop. When he picks the iPod back up, he tells his listeners he hasn't recorded in a while and gives a compressed version of what happened, then moves on. That's an editorial decision: the worst stretch of the book is exactly the part the aliens don't get. He's not confused about what happened. He understands it well enough to decide it doesn't belong on a record meant to represent Earth.
