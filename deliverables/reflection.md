# Written reflection (no AI)

I sought out to improve my ability to collect and make decisions based off feedback. I chose to drop the third twist of my game (knights vs knaves) based off feedback from Jim and Rhazes but ignored the rest of their feedback about the game having too many layers. This turned out to be a good choice because after the game was built I received very positive feedback about the game structure. I can still continue to improve upon taking feedback because Jim and Rhazes were correct that the game was slightly too fast paced even for the children. I also learned that I need to be slightly more in the loop because I accepted a recommendation to make the categories strange and unrelated to the game because I wasn't paying attention. Additionally, I can continue to build on my prompting skills including the specificity of my requests because the feedback shifted from the game being too short to too long meaning that I overshot the middle ground by being too vague. With another 2 weeks I would continue playing around with the lengths of each phase until the timing feels good (backed up by feedback) and continue to train the bots to be more convincing.
